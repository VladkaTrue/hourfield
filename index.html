Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch: no ads, no subscription, no tracking.

Will it drain my battery?

The clock only redraws while hands move and rests in between. For the night, keep the iPhone on the charger.

Does it change my screen brightness?

No. Night mode dims inside the app only.

Does the timer work with the phone locked?

Yes. Focus and breaks keep real time, and you get a notification at every change.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The alarm needs iOS 26 or later.

Which languages?

English and Russian, with one more language in every update.
